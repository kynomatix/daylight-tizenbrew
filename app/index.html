THE EVERYDAY VERSION

Your home’s energy, explained

Panels making
Electricity your solar panels are producing right now. It changes with sunlight and cloud cover.
Home using
Power the household is using right now. Switching an appliance on or off changes this number.
Battery full
How full your battery is, like a phone battery. It is not an estimate of hours remaining.
Back to the grid
Electricity leaving your home for the electricity network. “From the grid” means electricity coming in.

What is kW? How fast electricity is being made or used right now. 1 kW is 1,000 watts. These readings do not tell us the bill or guarantee that another appliance will run entirely on solar.

Clouds affect your solar

Cloud passing over your panels can reduce how much electricity they make. Thin cloud may only soften the sunlight; thicker cloud can cause a bigger dip.

Satellite imagery shows cloud patterns over a broader area. It cannot tell us exactly how much sunlight reaches your roof.
